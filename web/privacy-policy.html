Loopd Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Who We Are

Loopd ("we", "our", or "us") provides a free music streaming mobile application for Android and iOS. You can reach our privacy team at support@loopd.app.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account Data: If you register or authenticate via email or Google Sign-In, we store your email address and display name.
  • Music Preferences: Language preferences and followed artists to personalize recommendations.
  • Listening History: Recently played tracks and playlists stored in your personal library.
  • Diagnostics & Crashes: Anonymous crash reports and performance data via Firebase Crashlytics to resolve bugs.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do NOT sell your personal data.
  • We do NOT display third-party advertisements.
  • We do NOT build advertising profiles or share personal info with data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

We work with established partners to provide our services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, cloud database, and crash diagnostics.
  • Music Content Providers: JioSaavn and Jamendo for streaming metadata and audio streams under non-commercial licenses.

5. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access, rectify, or permanently delete your personal data at any time. You can delete your account and associated data directly in the app via Settings > Account > Delete Account, or submit a request on our web portal at /delete-account.

6. Children's Privacy

Loopd is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 13.
