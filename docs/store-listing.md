## Full Description
Loopd is your free, ad-free music streaming companion designed for pure audio lovers. Discover millions of tracks, curated playlists, and trending hits without disruptions.

### Highlights:
- **Zero Ads, Zero Distractions**: Listen to music the way it was meant to be heard—uninterrupted and completely free.
- **Instant Playback**: Tap any song to immediately dive into the full-screen player with lyrics and high-definition album art.
- **Background Media Controls**: Full lock-screen and notification shade playback controls that stay with you wherever you go.
- **Smart Recommendations**: Choose your favorite languages and artists for a Home feed tailored exclusively to your taste.
- **Curated Collections**: Browse by genre, mood, trending charts, and new releases with seamless 60 FPS navigation.
- **Offline Cache**: Lightning-fast caching for quick loading and smooth streaming on any connection.

Loopd respects your privacy and does not sell your personal data or track you for advertisements.
